# ScroBot

A macro bot and a pack of toggleable mods for Geometry Dash, all in one menu.

**Open the menu:** press **Tab** on PC (rebindable in the mod settings), or tap **Scro** in the pause menu on mobile. Tap an option's name to read what it does. Use **Find** (Global tab) to search every option.

Works on **Windows**, **Android 64-bit** and **Android 32-bit**.

## Replay (macro bot)
- **Record** a run, **Save** it, **Load** it from the list and **Play** it back.
- Inputs are tied to the physics step, so playback does not depend on FPS.
- Player position is corrected on every input. Works with practice-mode checkpoints.
- Macro-assisted runs are always marked as test runs.

## Global
- **Speedhack** (0.1x to 3x), **No Clip**, **Safe Mode**
- **Freeze Player** and **Frame Step**
- **Screen Record** with game sound (Android), open macro and data folders

## Player
- **Jump Hack** (jump in mid-air), **No Rotation**, **Solid Wave Trail**

## Level
- **Auto Practice**, **Practice Music Sync**, **Show Level Info**

## Visuals
- **Hitboxes**, **No Death Effect**, **No Glow**, **No Particles**, **Hide UI**
- **Low Detail Mode 2**: draws only objects near the screen, hiding passed objects and switching on upcoming ones. Everything comes back when you die or restart.

## Labels
- **FPS**, **CPS**, **Run Time**, **Clock**, **Frame**, adjustable label opacity
- **Click Analyzer** shows which physics step each click lands on

## FPS Boost
- Force **120 to 1000 FPS**, **Boost Frames** (click between frames), **Zero-Delay CBF** (Android), **Click Precision** (Android), **Frame-Time Diagnostics**

## Utility
- **Quick Restart** button in levels, **Auto Save**

## Settings
Menu colour, accent colour and font are in the mod settings on the Geode mod page.

## Safety
Cheat-like options (No Clip, Jump Hack, Speedhack, Freeze, Frame Step, Boost Frames and macro playback) always make completions count as test runs, so nothing is submitted to the servers. Macros are for practice, testing and showcases, and should not be presented as legitimate completions.
